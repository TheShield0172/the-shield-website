Where the product stands

The public website currently offers free FOIA information, local drafting and blank worksheets. The separate encrypted Case Vault is a fictional-data test application. Paid Plus and AI subscriptions, account management, customer billing, and production storage are not active.

We are publishing the proposed policy framework ahead of launch so visitors can evaluate the approach. It is not yet an operative agreement for paid customers and does not imply independent legal review or that real evidence handling is approved.

Commercial launch requirements

Before taking any payment we must identify the legal business and verified support channel, implement online cancellation and refunds, send billing acknowledgments and annual reminders, confirm taxable transactions, finalize a paid-service privacy notice, and independently review the sensitive-data product. No live payment system has been activated.

Relevant primary sources: Federal online subscription disclosures and cancellation (ROSCA); Michigan breach notification requirements. Michigan SB 535 is a bill under consideration, not treated here as enacted law.
