THE SHIELD • POLICY FRAMEWORK • NOVEMBER 16, 2026

Subscription, Renewal & Cancellation Policy

Clear pricing, automatic-renewal disclosures, and an uncomplicated exit for future subscribers.

Prelaunch disclosure: No paid subscriptions exist, no checkout is available, and the encrypted Case Vault is not approved for real confidential records. This document describes the proposed customer terms for a future launch; it does not create an active subscription, entitlement, or refund obligation today. Final legal-entity details, functioning support and cancellation channels, and legal/operational review are required before the policies take effect.

1. Proposed billing plans

THE SHIELD's planned paid tiers are Shield Plus and Shield AI. Amounts below are U.S. dollars before applicable taxes; no plans are currently for sale.

PlanMonthlyAnnual, prepaidAnnual savings
Shield Plus$6.99 each month$69.90 every 12 months$13.98 (two months)
Shield AI$12.99 each month$129.90 every 12 months$25.98 (two months)

Annual pricing equals ten monthly payments for twelve months of access, a 16.7% saving against twelve monthly payments. Annual subscribers pay the full annual amount upfront. Planned monthly AI usage limits apply equally to monthly and annual AI subscriptions. Exact included AI usage must be stated before checkout; it is not defined or offered yet. The free public FOIA guide and blank starter kit do not require a subscription.

2. Consent and first purchase

A paid subscription will begin only after the customer affirmatively chooses a plan and explicitly agrees to recurring charges. Before collecting payment details, the checkout must plainly show the merchant's legal identity, amount, billing interval, taxes, renewal date, service limits, cancellation method, and refund terms. No preselected consent checkbox, hidden trial conversion, or charge without express informed consent is permitted.

A receipt and copy or durable link to the agreed terms must be delivered after purchase. No trial or introductory billing will be offered unless its specific price, expiry, and opt-out terms are separately published and implemented.

3. Automatic renewals

Unless canceled, monthly subscriptions renew approximately one month after the first paid billing date and each month thereafter; annual subscriptions renew approximately 12 months after the first charge and then annually. Customers must be shown their exact next charge date and amount in a working account-billing area and on receipts.

For annual plans, we intend to email a renewal reminder at least 30 days before the next annual charge with the price, date, cancellation instructions and access to the billing page. This is a planned service obligation, not an email system currently operating. We will not charge for a renewal if cancellation was received before that renewal charge was processed.

Material pricing changes will be disclosed in advance (target: at least 30 days for annual renewals and at least 15 days for monthly renewals) with an easy way to decline by canceling. Prices will not change partway through a prepaid term. Any law requiring longer notice or additional consent will control.

4. Cancel easily

When paid plans launch, subscribers must be able to cancel online without calling anyone, giving a reason, or going through retention offers. The planned path is Account → Billing → Cancel subscription, with a direct cancellation option available through the payment provider's authenticated customer portal. It must clearly confirm cancellation and the last day of paid access.

Cancel any time. Cancellation stops future recurring charges and ordinarily leaves access available until the end of the paid term. It does not itself issue a refund; the separate Refund Policy governs refund eligibility. No early cancellation penalty will be charged. If a valid cancellation request is received before renewal, that renewal must not occur. If a technical error causes a charge after timely cancellation, we will reverse the erroneous charge.

A functioning customer-support fallback for customers unable to access the portal must be available before any paid subscriptions start. No cancellation portal or subscription-support mailbox exists for this preview yet; therefore purchases remain disabled.

5. Export and loss of access

Customers should export authorized personal records before their paid access ends. Paid data portability, retention after cancellation, and deletion options must be implemented and stated in the service-specific privacy policy before a paid launch. The present local browser beta is not reliable cloud storage; clearing browser data or losing a passphrase can cause permanent loss.

Automatic subscription cancellation will not itself authorize us to retain, upload, analyze or disclose confidential agency records. Restricted law-enforcement/CJIS, privileged, health or litigation materials are excluded from the current test product.

6. Billing problems and governing requirements

Before launch, THE SHIELD must identify the legal seller, a verified billing-support channel, the payment descriptor, any sales-tax obligations, and an operational method for processing cancellations and refunds. There is no service mailbox to contact for billing today because there are no paying subscribers.

These customer-friendly commitments are designed to align with the federal Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act (15 U.S.C. § 8403) and to anticipate state automatic-renewal obligations. Applicable law takes precedence. Read the federal statute.
