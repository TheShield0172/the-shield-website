Acceptable Use, Data Safety & Legal Disclaimer

A plain-language boundary separating public free tools from the unapproved encrypted vault.

Prelaunch disclosure: No paid subscriptions exist, no checkout is available, and the encrypted Case Vault is not approved for real confidential records. This document describes the proposed customer terms for a future launch; it does not create an active subscription, entitlement, or refund obligation today. Final legal-entity details, functioning support and cancellation channels, and legal/operational review are required before the policies take effect.

1. Allowed uses today

  • Use the free Michigan FOIA guide to learn about request, denial, fee and status procedures; independently verify current statutes and agency procedures.
  • Create an editable Michigan FOIA draft in your own browser; you are solely responsible for the filing and verifying any dates or legal arguments.
  • Print blank Career Documentation Starter Kit worksheets and use them only for lawful personal records you are authorized to possess.
  • Explore fictional public demonstrations; use the encrypted test workspace with invented records only.

2. Prohibited or restricted activities

  • Never put actual agency investigative records, CJIS data, restricted personnel documents, protected health information, classified or privileged materials, legal-hold evidence or active-case evidence into the experimental vault.
  • Do not use THE SHIELD to falsify records, impersonate people, bypass government access rules, publish private personal information, intimidate or harass, or interfere with lawful record retention.
  • Do not assume you own a record simply because you saw it at work. Confirm lawful possession, confidentiality, employer policies and retention requirements before copying anything.
  • Never publish passwords, financial account details, private contact information or substantive personal case information in public GitHub feedback or security issues.

3. FOIA and legal-content disclaimer

Michigan FOIA procedures differ from federal FOIA and from the laws of other states. A template cannot establish whether a record is disclosable, whether an exemption is valid, when a request was legally received, or the applicable court deadline. THE SHIELD is not an attorney or law firm. If a statutory deadline or dispute matters, consult an appropriate licensed professional.

The FOIA guide links to the Michigan FOIA request rules and related statutes. You must independently check them.

4. Encryption, backups and authentication limitations

The local beta encrypts records in browser storage; it has no independent audit, organizational access controls, trustworthy court-grade timestamps, verified provenance, or assured persistence. An authorized passphrase holder can alter local data; cryptographic hashes do not establish a lawful chain of custody. Browser extensions, compromised devices and malicious future website JavaScript remain risks.

Use test-only passphrases and fictional data. Never rely on browser storage as your only copy, and do not assume restoration or mobile compatibility is guaranteed.

5. Reporting problems safely

If you find a security flaw, do not publish sensitive exploit details, real records, credentials, or personal case facts in an issue. See the public security-reporting guidance; use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting option if it is available or request a secure contact path without including exploit details. There is no paid bug-bounty program.

Account-support, privacy-request and incident-response channels must be verified before paid launch.
