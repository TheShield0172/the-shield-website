Terms of Use & Proposed Paid Service Terms

What customers may expect from the free preview and what would apply before a future paid launch.

Prelaunch disclosure: No paid subscriptions exist, no checkout is available, and the encrypted Case Vault is not approved for real confidential records. This document describes the proposed customer terms for a future launch; it does not create an active subscription, entitlement, or refund obligation today. Final legal-entity details, functioning support and cancellation channels, and legal/operational review are required before the policies take effect.

1. Who operates the service and when these terms apply

THE SHIELD is currently a public pilot project. Its operating legal entity, registered commercial name, physical business contact information and private support channels have not yet been finalized for a paid launch. This webpage does not identify an authorized merchant or offer any paid subscription for purchase.

The free website and FOIA tools are available to visitors on an as-available, educational basis. Detailed paid service terms below are a prelaunch framework and must be confirmed and presented for acceptance when paid accounts are implemented.

2. Users and account access

Planned paid accounts will be limited to individuals aged 18 or older, with geographic eligibility confirmed before launch. Each user must safeguard their login and separate encryption passphrase. An employer, agency, department or legal professional is not a party to a user's subscription unless separately authorized in writing.

The free current website provides no user accounts and no server-side account recovery. We do not promise that a browser-only test vault is a safe place to store sensitive information.

3. Permitted use, ownership and records

Users retain whatever rights they hold in their own lawfully possessed, original records. THE SHIELD does not acquire ownership of a user's permitted personal content merely because it is processed through a future service. Users are responsible for having lawful authority to use, export and retain each record, and for following employer, legal-hold, confidentiality and retention rules.

The brand, website and original protectable software materials remain with their respective rights holders. The website's public source-code visibility does not grant an open-source license. The current free blank worksheets may be printed and completed for personal lawful use; redistribution, resale or branding changes are not authorized by this page.

4. Sensitive information and unlawful use

Do not input confidential investigative files, CJIS-controlled data, personnel files, privileged legal communications, sensitive health information, criminal history, active litigation evidence, classified military information, or materials restricted by law or employer policy into THE SHIELD's experimental vault. Do not use the site to harass anyone, fabricate evidence, impersonate others, circumvent access controls, break the law, or generate misleading legal claims.

The pilot may be changed, temporarily unavailable or withdrawn. Current demo pages contain fictional material and should never be treated as authenticated evidence.

5. Not legal advice or an evidence custodian

FOIA templates and source links are informational aids, not legal representations. THE SHIELD does not act as a law firm, attorney, fiduciary, document custodian, records-disclosure coordinator or court-certified evidence repository. You must verify facts, deadlines, statutes and the intended recipient before filing any document, and seek legal counsel for material rights or litigation deadlines.

Cryptographic checks and ordinary timestamps in the test app do not prove independent authorship, chain of custody, record authenticity, admissibility or legal compliance.

6. Proposed subscriptions and changes

Future paid access, if launched, will be governed by the Subscription Policy, Refund Policy, a complete service-specific Privacy Notice, and the actual terms explicitly accepted at checkout. Charges and renewals cannot begin without informed consent. We will publish material term changes in advance; changes will not retroactively reduce a paid term without legal justification and appropriate customer rights.

No arbitration provision, class-action waiver, forced publicity license, or contractual cap on legal consumer remedies is being imposed by this prelaunch draft. Any later dispute-resolution clauses require independent review and clear notice before assent.

7. Availability, complaints and legal rights

Reasonable security and service reliability are design goals, not warranties of perfect security, permanent availability, data recovery or favorable legal outcomes. Nothing on this page limits rights or remedies that applicable consumer, privacy, negligence, or other law prohibits limiting. The service will need operational complaint handling, verified legal identity and support contact before any paid customer enrollment.

For this free pilot, do not post sensitive complaints or personal documents to public GitHub issues. See the Policy Center for current status and official references.
