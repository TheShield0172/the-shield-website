HOW WE DECIDE WHAT'S NEXT

Build small. Test with real people. Spend only after demand.

We start with no-cost tools people can actually use. We measure voluntary feedback and direct conversations, improve what doesn't work, and only consider a commercial launch after the appropriate security, privacy and legal work is done.

1. Try itOpen the guide or print the starter kit. Neither requires an account.
2. Give feedbackGitHub reaction votes are public and optional. Don't post real workplace disputes.
3. Check valueWe distinguish casual clicks from people willing to test and eventually pay.
4. Release responsiblyNo paid evidence vault until security and legal safeguards are in place.

We do not use third-party advertising pixels or analytics scripts here. Hosting may log ordinary HTTP request metadata. Feedback posted on GitHub is governed by GitHub's policies and is public.
