Refund & Payment Correction Policy

A defined, fair process for first-time purchases, renewals, billing mistakes and service problems.

Prelaunch disclosure: No paid subscriptions exist, no checkout is available, and the encrypted Case Vault is not approved for real confidential records. This document describes the proposed customer terms for a future launch; it does not create an active subscription, entitlement, or refund obligation today. Final legal-entity details, functioning support and cancellation channels, and legal/operational review are required before the policies take effect.

1. First purchase — 14-day guarantee

For a future paid launch, customers may request a full refund of the initial subscription charge within 14 calendar days after the first monthly or annual purchase. This applies to both Shield Plus and Shield AI. The refund request also cancels future renewal charges; prepaid access ends when the refund is processed. There is no requirement to explain why the customer is requesting a first-purchase refund.

A refund guarantee is available once per customer for an initial subscription purchase, not as a repeatedly renewable trial. Legal rights to refunds for unauthorized charges, defective services or applicable consumer-protection requirements remain unaffected.

2. Renewal refunds

Annual renewals: We will offer a full refund of the annual renewal charge if requested within 14 calendar days of the renewal transaction. Requesting this refund also ends the renewed paid term. Canceling before the annual renewal avoids the charge in the first place.

Monthly renewals: We will offer a full refund of a monthly renewal charge if requested within 7 calendar days of the renewal transaction. Requesting the refund ends the renewed paid term. A customer may cancel monthly renewal at any time to prevent future charges.

Outside the stated windows, a change of mind or lack of usage ordinarily does not qualify for a partial or prorated refund. We may voluntarily make an exception for a verified prolonged outage or billing error, and any mandatory legal right to refund or cancellation takes precedence.

3. Duplicate, unauthorized or incorrect charges

Duplicate charges, billing after a timely cancellation, incorrect prices, and charges without authorization must be investigated and corrected. Where a charge is erroneous or unauthorized, we will issue the appropriate full reversal or refund irrespective of the ordinary windows, subject to verifying the transaction and applicable law.

Customers retain their rights to contact the payment provider or card issuer and to pursue applicable charge disputes. Customers should not submit full card details, passwords, confidential case files or sensitive documents in a support message.

4. How a refund will be requested

When checkout becomes available, a working Account → Billing → Request a refund route or an authenticated payment-provider support path must be implemented. A verified private billing-support address will also be published. Those mechanisms have not been built or activated, so these are the proposed operating procedures rather than instructions for an available purchase.

For approved requests, the target is to initiate the refund to the original payment method within five business days after approval. The payment provider or bank may require additional time for funds to appear. Receipts and refund confirmations should be sent to the customer's verified email address. No cash-equivalent gift card, store credit substitution, or additional purchase is required.

5. Taxes, discounts, and free products

A refund of a paid transaction includes refundable taxes and associated charges as required by law and the payment processor's handling. The annual two-month discount is reflected in the amount actually charged; we do not refund the undiscounted monthly-equivalent amount. The current free website, FOIA Desk and printable starter kit have no purchase price and therefore do not produce a monetary refund.

This policy will become effective only when the legal merchant, real payment workflow, support, notices, and compliance reviews are complete. Until then no refunds can be requested because THE SHIELD does not accept payments.
