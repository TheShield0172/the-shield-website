## 14-day test, budget $0

1. **Days 1–2:** Open each page from a phone and laptop. Print/save the free starter kit and draft a fictional FOIA example. Resolve any issues without handling real documents.
2. **Days 3–7:** Share the starter-kit link privately with 10 relevant people (outside agency systems): a mixture of police, fire, EMS, security, public-safety support or employees who actually use FOIA. No pressure; ask them to test with fictional data only. Don't imply agency endorsement.
3. **Days 8–10:** Share the same link with 10 more appropriate people or in a relevant community that permits member resources. Avoid spamming, misleading paid claims and agency-controlled channels.
4. **Days 11–14:** Check public GitHub feedback [free tools](https://github.com/TheShield0172/the-shield-website/issues/6), [future Plus](https://github.com/TheShield0172/the-shield-website/issues/7), [future opt-in AI](https://github.com/TheShield0172/the-shield-website/issues/8). Reactions require a free GitHub account. They are imperfect signals and **not** purchases, unique visits or representatively sampled respondents.
5. **Evaluate:** Did at least five people actually try the guide or template? Did at least three identify a concrete repeat-use problem? Did anyone, unprompted, ask whether a paid security-reviewed service is available? Document results without real case details. If not, revise the value proposition before spending anything.

## What counts as evidence of demand

**Weak:** page views, GitHub reactions, likes and compliments. They do not show unique users or purchasing commitment.

**Better:** named or identifiable voluntary testers who used the free tools and gave specific usability feedback (keep private contact details outside the public repository); repeat usage; genuine unscripted questions about when a reviewed paid version becomes available.

**Strongest:** legally compliant actual purchases of an independently reviewed, deliverable product—but **no preorders, payments, or paid evidence service** while checkout and security/legal requirements are unresolved.
