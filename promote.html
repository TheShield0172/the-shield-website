Agency-centered by design

A structured assessment. Not a personality quiz.

We are exploring a process in which every candidate faces comparable job-related problems and assessors document observable behaviors—not impressions or a black-box AI ranking.

01

Define the assignment

The agency and qualified experts identify the rank's essential tasks and supervisory competencies through job analysis.

02

Build realistic exercises

Develop standardized scenarios and decision paths with equivalent difficulty, expert-reviewed answer guides and accommodations.

03

Observe the candidate

Capture reasoning, communication, prioritization and leadership decisions under consistent administration rules.

04

Review evidence

Trained human assessors apply behaviorally anchored rubrics, document ratings, reconcile discrepancies and provide auditable results.

Built for agency confidence

Every promotion decision deserves a defensible process.

Standardized, not improvised

Documented job-related dimensions, administration protocols and candidate instructions.

Human judgment stays central

AI may assist scenario development or administrative review; it must not independently make employment decisions.

Evidence over guesswork

Traceable assessor notes, calibration, accommodations and review processes are part of the envisioned product.

Selection-use safeguard

This is not a ready-to-purchase promotional exam. Before an agency uses PROMOTE for real employment decisions, it will need appropriate job analysis and validation, legal and labor review, accessible administration, security protections, qualified assessors and adverse-impact monitoring. The requirements vary by jurisdiction and agency.
