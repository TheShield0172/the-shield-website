Artwork guide

Preferred: SVG. Convert text to paths, embed images, remove unused layers and keep vector artwork simple. DXF is suitable for line art; closed paths help with filled engraving.

Raster artwork: PNG (transparent background preferred), JPEG or BMP. At least 300 pixels per inch at the finished size; 600 preferred for fine detail. For an 8 × 2 inch full plate, aim for 2400 × 600 pixels or higher.

Use original files, sharp edges and good contrast. Avoid screenshots, blurry photos and upscaled low-resolution logos. Color images can be reviewed for a suitable engraving conversion.

Two files maximum, 10 MB combined. Complex SVGs over 1 MB may require simplification. Do not submit ZIP archives, executable files, confidential records, credentials, or unauthorized insignia.
