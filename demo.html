Sample issue-check

Potential issue: Are the withheld records adequately described and supported by applicable exemptions?

What the assistant should do: Identify which exemption the public body cited, link to the controlling source, and separate verified facts from unresolved questions.

What it should not do: Assume retaliation, misconduct, or that an exemption is invalid without adequate evidence and legal analysis.

Official starting point: Michigan FOIA, MCL 15.240.

Illustrative appeal structure

RE: APPEAL OF PARTIAL DENIAL — [Public body / request reference] This correspondence is an appeal of the partial denial identified in the response dated [date]. The decision withheld certain requested records on the stated basis of [cite exact exemption]. I request review and reversal to the extent records may be disclosed under applicable law. Please provide the written appeal determination required by the applicable procedure, and identify any additional non-exempt, reasonably segregable information that may be released. [Requester contact information]

Illustrative language only. It is not tailored to any real denial, and does not validate appeal dates, grounds, or the proper recipient.
