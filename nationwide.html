What to check in your state guide

1. Who can request?

Residency, requester status, agencies covered, and whether your requested record is public.

2. How to request?

Required request details, permitted submission methods, copying fees and response deadlines.

3. If denied?

Available administrative review, appeal procedures, time limits and court remedies. Do not assume the same rules apply in every state.

For public safety employment issues: A public-records request is not a substitute for requesting your own personnel file through an applicable employment law, collective bargaining agreement, civil-service rule, agency policy, or attorney discovery. Some records may be confidential or exempt.
