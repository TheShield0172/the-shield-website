Your documents, your choice

Designed to keep your records yours.

Our goal is client-side encryption with user-controlled backups to supported personal storage providers. Provider sign-in, automated synchronization and recovery still require implementation and security testing.

01. You control the keysUse a separate encryption passphrase. We must design recovery carefully without secretly copying plaintext.
02. You choose storageLocal and user-selected encrypted backups first; restricted-access cloud integration is planned.
03. AI is opt-inA document would only be sent for AI processing after a clear warning and explicit permission.
04. A source, not a verdictAI explanations must cite policies and laws and identify uncertainty. Attorneys remain essential.

Important: The private vault prototype is not approved for sensitive evidence, privileged records or confidential agency information. The public demo does not accept or store personal case files.

Common questions

Answers before you trust us.

Can my employer see my case?

The goal is employee-controlled access, but security claims require third-party review. Do not use the current prototype for real employment disputes.

Will it tell me a chief or investigator broke the law?

No. It should identify possibly relevant sources, conflicting facts and questions for professional review—not make unverified accusations or issue legal conclusions.

Can I connect Google Drive or iCloud?

User-directed encrypted backup works in the local beta. Full provider account syncing is not yet released. Apple Files/iCloud has a different integration model.

Will it automatically send a FOIA?

Not currently. The free Michigan FOIA Desk now creates editable requests and appeals locally. You must verify the agency, records, exemptions, and deadlines and file through appropriate channels. Open the free FOIA Desk.
