Privacy Notice

What the public THE SHIELD website does with information today, and what it does not do.

Current preview only. This notice describes the free public website, fictional demo, Michigan FOIA Guide and Desk, blank starter kit and founding-tester feedback links. It does not cover a future paid account, cloud evidence vault, production data storage or AI document-processing service. Those functions are not live. A complete service-specific privacy notice must be approved before any such launch.

1. Who this notice covers

THE SHIELD is the name of the in-development project. Its verified paid-service legal entity and designated private privacy-support contact have not yet been published. No paid accounts or payment processing are available on the public preview. The operator must identify the legal merchant and a private contact channel before a commercial launch.

2. What you enter and where it goes

  • Michigan FOIA Guide: Scenario selections are evaluated locally in your browser and are not sent to THE SHIELD servers or permanently stored by the guide.
  • FOIA Desk: Your typed request and appeal details are assembled into a draft on your device. The page does not send the draft to THE SHIELD servers or automatically file a request. Copy, print and download actions can place unencrypted data on your clipboard, device or print spool.
  • Optional local test workspace: When you choose to create it, the browser stores an encrypted package in IndexedDB on that device. The passphrase and decrypted key are not intentionally transmitted or stored on THE SHIELD servers. Browser extensions, device compromise and scripts running on the active page remain risks. This experimental local workspace is not approved for confidential, privileged or real litigation evidence.
  • Blank Starter Kit: The site provides blank printable worksheets. It does not collect text you later write on your own downloaded or printed copy.
  • Fictional demo: Demonstration content is prewritten and fictional.
  • Founding-tester voting: Links lead to public GitHub issues. Any comments or reactions made there are public or account-associated and subject to GitHub's own privacy terms. THE SHIELD does not receive your GitHub password. The optional no-login suggestion form sends general product feedback directly from your browser to Formspree when you choose to submit it for spam screening, an email notification to our receiving inbox, and storage subject to Formspree's policies. The form asks for a broad professional field, tool used, priority, suggestion and optional price interest; your email is optional. Formspree may process IP and technical metadata. On its free tier, Formspree currently limits submission archive retention to 30 days. There are no uploads, and this is not a secure channel for actual cases or restricted material. The feedback form does not automatically post messages to GitHub.

3. Hosting logs and third-party sites

Our web-hosting provider may process IP addresses, network-request timestamps, device/browser identifiers and security or diagnostic events as part of serving the website. Those logs are controlled by the hosting provider and its settings. We have not verified a precise log-retention period, so we do not promise immediate deletion of hosting metadata.

The website does not currently include an advertising pixel, behavioral analytics script, payment SDK, live AI endpoint or server-side user-login service. The no-login feedback page sends entered feedback to Formspree only when visitors explicitly submit the form. It does not automatically send form answers merely because a visitor opens the page. External links to GitHub, Michigan government resources or other sites operate under those sites' own privacy practices.

4. Browser storage, security and retention

The optional encrypted test records live in your browser's local storage environment until they are deleted, replaced, cleared by the browser or removed by the device. Local storage may be lost unexpectedly, and THE SHIELD cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Browser memory, malicious extensions, compromised devices, clipboard and plaintext downloads can expose information while the product is open.

THE SHIELD does not provide a production retention schedule, verified legal hold, agency policy compliance, forensic provenance, server-side backup, or guaranteed recovery in this free pilot. Never place restricted records in the test workspace. To remove the local copy, use the workspace's local-delete function or clear site data in your browser; exported backups must be deleted separately by whoever controls them.

5. Sharing, selling and AI processing

The current FOIA tools have no built-in server submission of draft content, no advertising or data-broker sale of draft contents, and no live AI document processing. We do not claim to control what hosting providers, browser or device providers, or third-party sites collect independently. The future paid service will require its own accurate, provider-specific privacy notice and express consent controls before any sensitive processing or AI upload can be considered.

6. Requests, security issues and changes

No account exists on the public pilot for online access, correction or deletion requests. To remove your local test records, you control your browser or exported backups. A verified private support and privacy-contact method must be published before paid enrollment; do not post personal data, confidential case material or contact details in public GitHub issues.

We may update this notice as the free pilot evolves and will display a revised date. For a future paid product, any material privacy changes affecting subscribers must be notified through an implemented customer communication system consistent with applicable law. For responsible vulnerability reporting, see the current security-reporting guidelines.

7. Applicable privacy and breach obligations

THE SHIELD will need to evaluate applicable federal and state consumer-privacy laws, service-provider arrangements, retention and data-breach notification requirements before activating accounts or paid storage. Michigan's data-breach notification duties are described in MCL 445.72. This is an informational source link, not a claim that the current test product is compliant or security certified.
