Preview privacy notice

This notice covers the public website, fictional interactive demo, and free Michigan FOIA Desk pilot. It does not cover a future paid service or the separate private evidence-vault prototype.

What the preview does

It serves static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The fictional demo contains only prewritten example data. The FOIA Desk lets you enter and edit real draft text locally in your browser, without sending that text to THE SHIELD servers. The optional encrypted test workspace uses your browser's IndexedDB storage and Web Crypto. Encrypted records remain on that device unless you export a backup yourself. There are no public-site user accounts, document uploads to our servers, live AI calls, subscription purchases or behavioral analytics scripts in this release.

Important local-storage and privacy limitations

Draft fields are readable in your browser while the page is open. Unencrypted downloads, printouts, and clipboard contents can be exposed to anyone with device access. An unlocked workspace is accessible to scripts running in that browser tab; browser extensions, compromised devices and other browser threats are outside this pilot's security controls. Local data may disappear if browsing data is cleared or the device fails. Passphrases cannot be recovered. The workspace is not independently security-audited or approved for confidential evidence, privileged material, or restricted agency records. Do not treat it as a forensic preservation system.

What hosting still involves

After deployment, the hosting platform may process and retain routine technical data (for example, IP addresses and request logs) according to its settings and policies. The website operator must verify those settings and publish complete business contact information before accepting real users.

What the preview cannot guarantee

This website is not a certified evidence vault, legal adviser, or protected attorney-client channel. The FOIA Desk is a local drafting aid; it cannot assess all legal issues, verify deadlines, or send requests. Do not enter restricted agency records, privileged content, or actual litigation evidence into its experimental workspace.

Before a commercial launch

A proper service privacy notice, terms, data retention policy, incident-response plan, provider contracts, independent security testing, and legal review are still required.
